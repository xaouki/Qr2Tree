Animated 3D tree QR code generator

Qr2Tree turns a normal scannable QR into a living visual experience inspired by trees and nature. Create an animated QR code, tree QR, 3D QR art, QR GIF, QR video or custom QR image directly in your browser.

The exported tree combines the animated visual and the QR. The separate QR export gives you a clean standard code for printing, while the tree export is designed for social media and creative presentation.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the QR actually scannable?

Yes. The QR is generated with a standard QR library and high error correction. Always test a final creative export before publishing or printing.

What does PNG · Tree + QR contain?

It is the first visual form: the moving tree design is captured as a still image with the QR integrated into the composition. You can also download the clean QR separately.

Can I export animation?

Yes. Qr2Tree can record the animated canvas as WebM video and can create a short GIF in browsers that support the required canvas APIs.

Does Qr2Tree upload my content?

No server upload is required for QR generation, logo processing or the visual animation.
