FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Qr2Tree free?

Yes. The QR generator is free to use and downloads are generated in your browser.

Can I turn an image into a QR code?

Yes. You can encode an image URL or add a small image logo to the generated QR. A QR code encodes data; it does not literally turn the binary image into a QR without encoding that image data or URL.

Will my QR code still scan?

Qr2Tree uses a standard QR generator and high error correction. Always test your final downloaded design with several phones before printing or publishing it.

Does Qr2Tree store my links or images?

No server upload is required for generation. Your browser handles the entered content and selected logo locally.
